Understanding this chord

G Augmented uses the scale degrees 1, 3, #5, measured from G. A voicing arranges those notes in a particular register. An inversion names which chord tone is lowest; it does not change the chord’s pitch classes.

An augmented triad has a major third and an augmented fifth: 1, 3, #5. Its semitone formula is 0–4–8. Its equal spacing creates enharmonically equivalent pitch sets with other roots.
