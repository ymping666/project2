One Note · privacy

Your practice stays here

This local edition has no accounts, analytics, advertising, cookies, or external asset requests. No practice answers are sent to a server.

Practice uses your browser’s localStorage to remember answers separately for each study. Records contain the study ID, a format version, and your chosen answer indexes. Storage can be unavailable in private browsing or restricted browser settings; the study still works for the current visit.

Use “Reset practice” on a study to remove its saved answers, or clear this site’s browser data to remove all records. Corrupt or outdated records are ignored safely. The local HTTP server can see the paths your browser requests as part of serving the files; it does not need your practice records.

Audio is generated in your browser after you press a play button. No microphone, audio upload, external recording, or network audio service is used.
