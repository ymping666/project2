One Note · privacy

Your practice stays here

This edition has no site accounts, added analytics, advertising, or third-party asset requests. The application does not set cookies or send practice answers to a server.

Practice uses your browser’s localStorage to remember answers separately for each study. Records contain the study ID, a format version, and your chosen answer indexes. Storage can be unavailable in private browsing or restricted browser settings; the study still works for the current visit.

Use “Reset practice” on a study to remove its saved answers, or clear this site’s browser data to remove all records. Corrupt or outdated records are ignored safely. The hosting provider may process page and asset requests, including ordinary request metadata, to serve the site. Browser-only practice records are not included in those requests. Hosting-provider logs are separate from the application’s local practice storage.

Audio is generated in your browser after you press a play button. No microphone, audio upload, external recording, or network audio service is used.
