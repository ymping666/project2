Understanding this scale

Eb Minor pentatonic uses the scale degrees 1, b3, 4, 5, b7, measured from Eb. The fretboard marks every matching pitch class from open strings through fret 12. The piano highlights the ascending register used in playback; other octaves contain the same pitch classes.

Minor pentatonic uses five degrees: 1, b3, 4, 5, b7. It omits degrees 2 and b6 from natural minor.

Piano notes use scientific pitch notation; C4 is middle C. Each key is one semitone from its neighbor.

Positions are selected by pitch and fret constraints. They are study diagrams, not verified fingering recommendations. No finger numbers or claims about ease of playing are implied.
