Understanding this chord

G Minor uses the scale degrees 1, b3, 5, measured from G. A voicing arranges those notes in a particular register. An inversion names which chord tone is lowest; it does not change the chord’s pitch classes.

A minor triad combines a root, minor third, and perfect fifth. Its semitone formula is 0–3–7.
